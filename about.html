About BAM-Web Crafters

We are a dedicated computer science student team passionate about showcasing the hidden geographical and cultural gems of the Sultanate of Oman.

Our Mission & Objectives

Our website, "Omani Hidden Treasures," is designed to bridge the gap between travelers and the lesser-known wonders across Oman's 11 governorates. Our core goals include:

Accurate Information

Provide accurate information, seamless navigation, and interactive exploration tools.

Sustainable Tourism

Promote sustainable local tourism and cultural appreciation.

Modern Standards

Provide verified data and direct Google Maps integration for travelers, adhering strictly to modern HTML and CSS web standards.
